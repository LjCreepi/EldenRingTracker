Elden Ring Tracker

Track your personal progress through Elden Ring on an interactive map of the Lands Between — toggle marker categories, and mark bosses, sites of grace, dungeons and pickups as done. The Character menu adds a by-hand inventory, an equipment loadout and a status sheet, styled after the in-game menus.

Character stats On the Status and Equipment screens, equip-load percentage, roll weight and Level are exact. HP, FP, stamina, poise, defences and resistances are approximations (marked “≈”), and weapon Attack Rating is not simulated — equipped weapons show their base values only. Don’t use these against a real save or a build calculator. Map symbols A small flag on a completed pin means an optional bonus step is still open — e.g. a found merchant whose Bell Bearing hasn’t been claimed yet. Open the pin again to deal with it.
