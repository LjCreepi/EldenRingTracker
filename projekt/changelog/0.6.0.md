# 0.6.0 — 2026-09-11

### Added

- **Weapon Attack Rating.** The Equipment screen's Weapon Build panel
  computes a real Attack Rating for the selected armament/shield from:
  - **Upgrade Level** (0–25 Standard / 0–10 Somber) and **Upgrade Path** —
    the multiplier curve for each is verified against real per-weapon
    attack-per-level data (Longsword for Standard, Moonveil for Somber). The
    Upgrade Path picker defaults from a hand-curated, non-exhaustive list of
    well-known Somber weapons and is always user-correctable.
  - **Two-Handing** — the exact +50% effective Strength bonus.
  - **Affinity** — selectable (any affinity, no legality check) and shown,
    but doesn't change the computed number yet.
  - The attribute soft-cap curve behind the scaling contribution is a
    best-effort approximation of the real per-weapon formula (breakpoint
    locations are well-established; exact percentages are not verified the
    same way the upgrade curves are).
  - See `docs/adr/0010` for the full fidelity breakdown.
- **Wondrous Physick.** A new Physick Mix section on the Equipment screen:
  pick two Crystal Tears (from all 29 base-game ones), toggle the mix
  Active, and see its effect against your attributes and Attack Rating.
  Numeric effects are modeled for the four attribute-boosting Crystal Tears
  and the two instant-restore ones (verified via source lookups, catching a
  wrong 20%→50% FP-restore figure along the way); the rest show their real
  catalog effect text without a numeric delta yet. See `docs/adr/0009`.
- **Golden Seed / Sacred Tear tracking.** Completing one of their map markers
  now adds the Item to Inventory automatically (and removes it if
  un-completed) — no more manually keeping Inventory in sync with the map.
  A new Flask section on the Status screen tracks how many have actually
  been *Used* at a Site of Grace (vs. just collected), separate from raw
  Inventory quantity, and shows the resulting Flask Charges / Flask Potency.
  See `docs/adr/0008`.

### Fixed

- The item-detail panel's weapon stat block no longer says "scaling not
  simulated" — stale since Attack Rating started being simulated on the
  Equipment screen.
- The Equipment screen's upgrade-level stepper's "lower" button had an
  unregistered icon, logging an Ionicons console warning.

### Changed

- Item-detail's "Attack Power" heading is renamed "Base Attack" — it's the
  catalog's raw, unmodified value; the real computed number is now Attack
  Rating, shown separately in the Weapon Build panel.
